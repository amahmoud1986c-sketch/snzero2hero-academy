1) Overview & Business Value

Open component:AccountsContactsCasesCSM Workspace

CSM manages service for EXTERNAL customers (B2B / B2C). — الفرق الجوهري عن ITSM: العميل خارجي (شركة أو مستهلك)، وليس موظفًا داخليًا.

Core value:
- Case management end-to-end.
- Self-service via Customer Service Portal.
- Connect ITSM/FSM/Field for fulfillment.
- Proactive customer service (outages, notifications).

 gr.addQuery('escalation', '<', 3);

}
}

c.getNumericValue()) / 60000);
