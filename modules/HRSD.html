9) Best Practices & Common Mistakes

Open component:HR CasesHR ProfilesHR ServicesEmployee Center

Best practices:
- Configure HR Criteria carefully — data privacy is critical.
- Use COEs to separate sensitive data (Employee Relations).
- Use Lifecycle Events for multi-team journeys (onboarding).
- Surface everything through Employee Center.

Common mistakes:
- تجاهل HR Criteria → تسريب بيانات حساسة.
- استخدام ITSM incident بدل HR Case للطلبات الموظفية.
- خلط HR Profile بـ sys_user دون ربط صحيح.
