4.2 Day 2 — Data model workshop facilitation

Open component:TablesLeave RequestsEmployeesBalancesAudit

Ask:
1. What is the transaction record? (incident, case, asset, project)
2. What is the master record? (account, person, CI, profile)
3. What lookup records do you need? (categories, types, priorities)
4. What relationships matter? (parent-child, M:N)
5. What lifecycle states does each record have?

Output: 1-page ER diagram per table.
