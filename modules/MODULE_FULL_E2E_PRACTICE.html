English narrative you can demo across modules:

  1. Employee submits leave in HRSD scoped app (/esc).
  2. HR agent works case in /now/hr/agent — HR Criteria hides payroll fields from manager.
  3. IT receives onboarding task — catalog laptop (ITSM.md §16.6).
