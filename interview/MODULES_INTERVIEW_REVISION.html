Open component:ApplicationLeave Requests

Q: REQ vs RITM?
A: One REQ (order); each catalog item = one RITM; fulfillment = sc_task(s).

Q: SLA pause?
A: When incident On Hold — pause condition on task_sla.

Interview Q&A

Open component:ApplicationLeave Requests

Q: HAM vs SAM?
A: HAM = physical assets, stockroom, deploy/retire. SAM = licenses, entitlements, compliance, reclamation.

Q: ITAM vs CMDB?
A: ITAM = lifecycle/cost/stockroom/licensing. CMDB = configuration & service impact. Link via CI on asset.

Q: How does catalog request become an asset?
A: sc_req_item → procurement PO → receive → creates alm_hardware → deploy to user.
